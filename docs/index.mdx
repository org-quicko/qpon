Qpon is a self-hosted coupon management platform built for startups and engineering teams who need full control over their promotional workflows. Built on NestJS, PostgreSQL, and Angular, Qpon gives you a REST API, a management dashboard, and a TypeScript client SDK — all deployable in minutes with Docker.

## What you can build

Qpon handles the full lifecycle of promotional codes — from creation to redemption tracking. Typical use cases include:

- **E-commerce promotions** — percentage or fixed-amount discounts tied to specific products
- **SaaS trial offers** — private codes distributed to specific customers with per-customer redemption limits
- **Marketing campaigns** — budgeted campaigns with start/end windows and exhaustion tracking
- **Loyalty programs** — customer-specific codes with maximum redemption controls

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Deploy Qpon">
    Run Qpon with a single `docker compose up -d` command. Qpon bundles the API, frontend dashboard, and PostgreSQL database.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an organization">
    Use the setup wizard at `/setup` to create your super admin account and first organization. Everything in Qpon is scoped to an organization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your coupon catalog">
    Create coupons with discount rules, then organize them into campaigns with budgets and validity windows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Distribute and redeem">
    Generate coupon codes (public or private), assign them to customers, and call the redeem endpoint from your checkout flow.
  </Step>
</Steps>
